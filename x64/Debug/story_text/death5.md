The alarm rips me out of the dark.

No dream, no smooth fade. Just black and then my phone screaming in my ear.

Same ceiling. Same room. Same crack across the screen.

I drag myself into the kitchen.

There's a note on the table, folded under an empty mug.

Left early. Night shift again.
Food's in the fridge.

I read it once, and this cold little shiver runs down my spine.

I know these words.
Not just **Mom always writes something like this** more specific. Like I've already stood in this exact spot, in this exact light, with this exact headache.

My skull feels heavy, full of fog. No real memories, just some scraps of... something. A crash. A button. A line of text:

...chance to start over...

I look down to open the pill box.

Letters stare back at me from my wrist.

Thick black marker, still slightly uneven:

EMMA IS A WHORE

For a moment the kitchen tilts.
I did not see this name long time. Who even did this?


I don't remember writing this.
I don't remember anyone grabbing my arm this morning.
The only thing that comes is a half-image: brick wall, rough hands, laughter. Then it's gone again, like a dream that slips away as soon as you focus on it.
I am freaking out! Maybe I have drunk something yesterday or am I just crazy.

My head throbs harder.

I pull my sleeve down until the words disappear and swallow the pill dry.

One step at a time.
School first. Questions later.

The city, the people, the edge of town all of it feels wrong in a way I can't name. Every turn in the route hits like a scene I've already watched.

The school bus wheezes in the same spot. I climb in, walk to the back, sit in my usual place.

That crawling feeling runs up my neck.

I turn my head.

She's there.
Back corner, shoulder to the window, big headphones on. Breath on the glass. The shape of her profile, the way her fingers tap on her knee it's like someone hit replay on a memory I don't actually have.

Music leaks from under her headphones.
Just a faint beat.

Familiar.

Too familiar.

Something tightens in my chest. I stare at the seat in front of me and pretend I don't hear it.
I am not so brave after all, also this inscription pisses me off, it's a very strange morning.

In class, the headache turns into a low, steady drill.

The teacher fights with the projector. Desktop. Folders. Click. Glitch.

The video bursts onto the board.

Trashy music, ugly laughter, faces cut together in the worst way. And then her face, or what they did to it.

Emma.

My stomach knots.

The room reacts whistles, giggles, someone saying **no way, that's really her**. The teacher shouts, reaching for the keyboard.

The last frame freezes for a second.

Big white letters:

EMMA IS A WHORE

The same phrase burns on the board and under my sleeve at the same time.

My hand twitches. I tug the fabric up just enough to see the edge of the last word.

...HORE.

Exactly the same.

But I woke up with this.
Before first bell. Before the video. Before any of this **happened**.

My head spins.

The teacher finally turn off the image. The class shifts back into fake normal. I hear almost nothing after that.

The bell rings.

I'm packing my bag when three guys block the way. One leans on my desk, spinning a marker between his fingers.

**Don't run off after last bell,**. **We wanna talk. Don't you dare to run home or things will get worse.**

The marker cap is already off. The smell punches straight through the fog in my head.

I look at the marker.
I look at my covered wrist.
I look at his face.

Something inside finally clicks, not words, just a feeling:

How is this possible?.
