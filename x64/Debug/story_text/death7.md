The alarm punches through the dark.

Same ceiling. Same crack across the phone screen. Same heavy, swollen feeling in my head, like I've been thinking too hard in my sleep.

I drag myself to the kitchen.

Fridge hum. Clock ticking. Pale morning light.
Note on the table, mug on top.

> Left early. No night shift today, will be home at 6.
> Food's in the fridge.

I read it once, and my stomach does a small, stupid flip.

It's not just **Mom writes notes like this all the time**.
It's *this* note. Every line feels pre-read.

My head throbs in agreement.

I reach for the pill box.

Black letters flash at the edge of my vision.

I turn my wrist.

**EMMA IS A WHORE**

The words hit like a punch even though I don't really know *from where*. There's no clear scene attached, just a sour taste in my mouth and a tightness in my chest that feels old.

I push the sleeve higher.

There's more ink.

On the other forearm, cramped, all caps, definitely my handwriting:

**IF YOU SEE THIS, IT'S REAL.
YOU WENT BACK.
YOU FORGET.
GAME = RESTART.**

For a second the kitchen blurs.

I don't remember writing this.
At all.
Not a second of it.

But the pen strokes are mine. The weird way I draw my E's is there. The crooked spacing. The rushed seriousness.

**You forget,** the note says.

I swallow.

If I really don't remember doing this, then the idiot on my arm might actually be telling the truth.

The school bus, the day, all of it suddenly feel... optional.

I tug my sleeves down, and stand there, listening to the clock tick.

**Okay,** I tell the room. **Fine. Let's assume you're not crazy. For today.**

I text school something about a headache and not coming. No explanation. No guilt. If the universe can rewind days, it can survive one truancy.

Today I stay here.

Today I figure out what the hell past-me was trying to say.

My room looks exactly like it always does: too small, too cluttered, too familiar. PC on the desk, chair not pushed in, the game icon sitting on the desktop, neon logo waiting.

I sit down slowly.

Pieces I can't quite see clearly poke at me from the edges of my thoughts:
monitor glow, impact, blackness, that one line from the hidden story:

*A chance to start over.*

I don't remember the whole chain. But my own handwriting is right there on my arm, insisting that the game did something.

I boot it.

Neon explodes into the dark of my room. The main track kicks in. I immediately start the level. Die instantly. I go straight to **Story**.

The same page I ignored a hundred times and, apparently, once didn't.

I scroll until I find it.

*Those who are stubborn enough to keep going,*
*and foolish enough to believe,*
*may get a chance to start over.*

If I read this yesterday  or whenever  and then woke up with my skin shouting **YOU WENT BACK**, that's not a coincidence I can just shrug off.

I lean back in the chair and close my eyes for a moment.

Emma.

Her name sits in my head like a stone. No clear pictures, just weight. The word on my wrist is about her. I don't need memories to know I hate seeing it there.

If starting over is real...
If this thing can actually pull me backwards...

It doesn't have to stop at one day.

I look at my arms again.

Marker with bad words about my love.
Marker from me, trying to warn myself.

If only the body comes along for the ride, then the body is the only place I can leave anything that survives.

Paper won't.
Messages won't.
Memory definitely won't.

My body will.

**Alright,** I whisper. **Then we use skin.**

On the inside of my left arm, under the first note, I start writing again, choosing every word like it costs me:

**YOU DON'T REMEMBER, BUT THIS IS TRUE.
TIME JUMPS BACK ONE MORNING WHEN YOU DIE IN THE GAME + PRESS RESTART.
ONLY YOUR BODY COME WITH YOU.
USE THIS TO GO FURTHER BACK.
DON'T WASTE LOOPS.**

And a big message:

**IT MAY SOUND INSANE, BUT YOU JUMP ONE DAY BACK IN TIME EVERY TIME YOU RESTART THE GAME YOU CREATED. SOMEHOW, AS IF BY MAGIC, THE WORDS WRITTEN IN THE STORY TURNED OUT TO BE THE TRIGGER OR SO I SUSPECT, FOR THE TIME JUMP. NOW, EVERY TIME YOU RESTART YOUR OWN GAME, YOU SINK ONE DAY INTO THE PAST. SINCE I'VE BEEN GIVEN THIS CHANCE, I'M OBLIGED TO USE IT TO SAVE HER. IF I WARN SOMEONE ABOUT HER ILLNESS EARLIER, EVERYTHING MIGHT GO DIFFERENTLY. SHE MIGHT LIVE. THE MAIN THING IS THAT MY PAST SELF KEEPS FOLLOWING THE INSTRUCTIONS, TRIES RESTARTING AGAIN, DOESN'T GIVE UP. NO MEMORIES ARE CARRIED OVER. JUST A FAINT HEADACHE AND EMPTINESS. IT FEELS LIKE EVERYTHING IS GOING THE WAY IT SHOULD, AND YET THESE MESSAGES ARE DRIVING ME CRAZY. PLEASE, IF THIS IS REAL, KEEP GOING.** 

signing it with today's date.

My hand shakes a little, but the letters come out readable.

I give myself one more section, even smaller, tighter:

**RULES FOR YOU (YES, YOU):

1. BELIEVE THE WRITING.
2. TEST, BUT DON'T STALL.
3. EVERY TIME YOU WAKE, ADD CLEAR NOTES.
4. REMEMBER: YOU LOVE HER. YOU'RE DOING THIS FOR HER.**

I cap the marker and stare at what I've done.

It looks insane.
It also looks like the only map I have.

I don't know how many **chances to start over** I'm going to get.
I don't know if I can really reach that far back.
I don't even know what I'll be able to change if I do.

But sitting here and pretending this is all a weird dream hasn't exactly made my life better so far.

So I do the only thing that makes sense:

Restart the level.
