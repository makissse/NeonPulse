The alarm yanks me out of sleep, and the first thing I feel is weight.

Not on my chest on my arms.

Kitchen. Note. Mug. Same pale light.

I don't finish reading. I already know what it says.

Instead I roll my sleeves up.

Both forearms are almost full now. Black ink, cramped, layered over itself. Some lines half-faded, some fresh and sharp. It looks like I fell asleep on a page from someone's crazy notebook.

Right wrist: the same sentence I don't want to think about.

EMMA IS A WHORE

Above it, in my handwriting:

CHECK THE DATE. IF IT MATCHES THE DAY OF THE SURGERY, IT'S TOO LATE TO CHANGE THAT.

What a funny joke?
Who even wrote this?

I slept for 3 hours, because today is the **operation** date. I want to visit Emma when it's all done.

Further up the arm:

YOU CAN'T STOP IT FROM HAPPENING NOW.
YOU TRIED (TRUST THIS).
NEW GOAL: GO BACK TO BEFORE THE OPERATION WAS EVEN SCHEDULED.
BEFORE THE HOSPITAL, IF POSSIBLE.

I have no memory of those tries it talks about.
Just a dull ache behind my eyes and the sense that whoever wrote this was very, very tired.

On the inside of my elbow, one more block of text:

DON'T TELL HER ABOUT THIS TODAY.
DON'T TELL HER PARENTS.
YOU'LL SOUND INSANE AND IT WON'T HELP.
IF YOU GO TO THE HOSPITAL, JUST BE THERE.
TONIGHT: USE THE GAME AGAIN. AIM FURTHER BACK.

I sit at the table and let it sink in.

I could pretend it's all some elaborate sleepwalking episode.
I could scrub it off and act like none of this makes sense.

But the dates keep lining up. The ink knows things I shouldn't.
Too much evidence. The more I read, the crazier I feel. I believe it all...

No, that's bullshit. Emma is what's important right now.

I go to the hospital like a normal boyfriend with shaking hands.

The day tastes like metal.

Waiting room chairs. Coffee that smells burned. Nurses walking past with that practiced, neutral face. Her parents sitting across from me, not looking at me, shoulders carved out of stone.

At some point a doctor appears, and the world shrinks to a corridor and a voice and a handful of words that don't land where they're supposed to.

**Complications.**
**Not the result we hoped for.**
**I'm sorry.**

I nod. I don't remember walking home.

That evening, my room feels like a cave someone left behind.

The monitor glows in the dark. The game icon waits. My arms itch under the dried ink.

On the inside of my left arm, I squeeze in one more line, hand shaking:

SURGERY WENT BAD.
THIS DAY CAN'T BE SAVED.
USE THE GAME. GO FURTHER BACK.
FIND A POINT WHERE THERE'S STILL TIME TO SAY SOMETHING.

I stare at it until the letters blur.

Then I start the game, throw myself into the first obstacle I see, and hit restart like I'm pushing against the whole universe.

Black.
