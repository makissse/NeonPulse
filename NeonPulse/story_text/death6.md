I don't go to classes, I'm running out of school and waiting for the bus at thr local restaurant.

It's 3 o'clock, heading to bus stop.
Cold air hits my face like a slap. It helps.

Taking the bus with no incidents.

Home feels wrong, like I've broken some invisible script.

Shoes off. Door shut. Silence.

I stand in the hallway for a second, listening to my own heartbeat, then head straight to the bathroom. Bright light, ugly tiles, mirror.

I roll my sleeve up.

**EMMA IS A WHORE**

The same letters from the video.
But I woke up with them *before* it played.

My reflection looks pale and confused.
Same as I feel.

**Okay,** I tell the guy in the mirror. **Let's pretend you're not completely insane for a second.**

Pieces, in order:

1. I remember sitting at my PC.
2. I remember the game.
3. I remember a death in a level I never die on.
4. I remember pressing restart.
5. ...black.

Then alarm.
Note on the table.
Bus. Girl. Video. Marker.

And the words already waiting on my skin this morning.

If I really went back in time, then whatever happened *before* that restart is gone from my head.

But not from my body.

The marker stayed.

**So if it happens again,** I mutter, **I'll probably lose this too. The knowing. Not the ink.**

The thought makes my stomach twist.

I don't have proof. I barely have fragments. Just a headache, a feeling, and a sentence on my arm that arrived out of order.

But if I sit here and do nothing, tomorrow will be the same shit on repeat. Bus, girl, video, marker, maybe worse.

If there's even a tiny chance that the game did this...

...I have to check.

I dig through a drawer until I find an old black marker of my own. The tip is dry at first, then starts to draw.

On my other forearm, higher up where a sleeve won't rub it off too fast, I write in crooked capital letters:

**IF YOU SEE THIS, IT'S REAL.
YOU WENT BACK.
YOU FORGET.
GAME = RESTART.**

It looks ridiculous.
Like something a crazy person would bring to a doctor.

But if I wake up again with no memory and *this* on my skin, at least the next version of me will start with more than a headache.

I cap the marker and drop it on the sink.

My hands are shaking a little as I sit down at my desk and wake the monitor. The game icon waits where it always waits.

**Round two,** I whisper. **Or three. Or whatever this is.**

Click.

Neon. Music. The level I could run in my sleep.

I don't even try to play properly this time.

I launch, take a few steps, and then steer straight into the first obstacle I see.

Crash.
Death screen.
Finger on restart.

**A chance to start over,** I hear in the back of my head.

I press the button.

The music cuts.
The image snaps to black.
The floor falls away.

And the world goes out again.
